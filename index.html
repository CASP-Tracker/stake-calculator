How this is calculated

  • Rates are the figures each exchange published on its own public pages on . They change often and some include promotions or balance tiers. Check the source link on each card before you stake.
  • Amounts in EUR assume the coin's price stays the same. Real value moves with the market. Pick the coin as the unit to see rewards in coins instead.
  • With compounding on, rewards are reinvested monthly for an APR figure. A figure the exchange calls APY already includes compounding, so it is used as published. Bybit pays simple interest that does not compound, so the switch has no effect on it.
  • Kraken publishes its staking rates before its commission (25% on bonded staking, 30% on flexible, which also earns on at most half of the stake). The calculator applies both and shows the published figure next to the net one.
  • Fixed-term products are assumed to renew at the same rate. Rewards shown are before tax and before any fee the exchange does not include in its published rate.
  • The licence line comes from the ESMA register as listed on CASP Tracker. Staking is not one of the ten MiCA services, so the exchange licence does not make a staking product regulated, guaranteed or insured. Each card says what its exchange states about the product.

This is an information tool, not financial or tax advice. Staking carries risk, including price loss, lock-up and exchange risk.
